Your seaside retreat

Comfort, calm, and the coast — all in one place

Imagine a three-bedroom, one-suite retreat where every room opens onto views of a pool and garden. Relax on one of four balconies, breathe in the sea air, and unwind at the shared pool surrounded by lawn — ideal for families and groups.

NorteSoul Miramar sits inside a quiet, private condominium just 100 metres from the beach, with a pharmacy, train station, bakery, market, butcher and restaurants all within easy walking distance. Perfect for a holiday, or a longer stay with all the comforts of home.
